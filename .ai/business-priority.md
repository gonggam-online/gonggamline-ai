# Business priority

The objective is actual sales, measurable profit, repeatable commerce automation, and stable monthly revenue of KRW 100,000,000. Completing software is a means, not the final objective.

- P0: financial, security, data, order, inventory, and production safety.
- P1: functionality that directly accelerates a real sale.
- P2: repeatable revenue and operational automation.
- P3: usability and scalability.
- P4: long-term optimization.

Prefer the smallest reliable implementation. Ask whether work accelerates a first sale, improves revenue/margin/capacity, prevents material risk, or can safely wait.

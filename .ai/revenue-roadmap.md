# Shortest revenue path

Product discovery → fresh identical-product delivered-price check → conservative full-cost profitability gate → approval → minimum-MOQ sample purchase → supplier/inspection verification → content generation → Coupang listing → inventory/order/fulfillment → realized revenue and profit measurement → scale.

Never purchase a resale sample to discover whether the product might be
profitable. Missing or failed pre-purchase economics stops procurement; only a
passing product may advance to a separately approved minimum sample purchase.

Prioritize the earliest blocked stage, while P0 safety outranks speed. Automate a stage only after its smallest reliable manual path is measurable.
